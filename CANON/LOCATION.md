# Location Profile

## The Hostel

**Name:** Luna Tribe Hostel
**Type:** Party Hostel / "Wellness-adjacent"
**Star Rating:** 3.5 stars
**Price Range:** 400-1200 THB per night ($12-35 USD)

### History
Opened in 2015 by a British expat couple who met at a Full Moon Party and never left. Originally positioned as "conscious partying" - yoga in the morning, raving at night. The wellness veneer has faded but the marketing remains. Three overdoses in the past two years, none fatal until now, all kept quiet.

### Architecture
- **Style:** Modern tropical with bamboo accents, Instagram-ready design
- **Floors:** 2 floors plus beachfront bungalows
- **Total beds:** 80 beds plus 12 private bungalows
- **Notable features:** Direct beach access, rooftop yoga deck, pool with swim-up bar, "healing garden" nobody uses

### The Death Location (Specific)
**Where exactly does the death occur?**
The beach about 50 meters from the hostel, in the soft sand between the hostel's fire pit area and the main Full Moon Party zone. The spot is semi-dark, lit only by distant fire spinners and the glow of the party. Priya's body lies in the tide line; the water doesn't reach her but it's close. A faded string of fairy lights marks the boundary between hostel and public beach.

## The City

**City:** Haad Rin, Koh Phangan
**Country:** Thailand
**Population:** ~3,000 permanent residents (swells to 30,000+ during Full Moon Party)
**Known for:** Full Moon Party, backpacker scene, yoga retreats, contrast of hedonism and wellness

### Geography
- **Climate:** Tropical - hot, humid, monsoon season June-October
- **Terrain:** Beach town on peninsula, surrounded by jungle hills
- **Weather during story:** Early December, dry season, hot days, warm nights, clear skies

### Culture
- **Language(s):** Thai (English widely spoken in tourist areas)
- **Religion(s):** Buddhism (temples on the island often visited by hungover tourists)
- **Customs relevant to story:** Thai mai pen rai attitude toward tourist excess, but genuine community concern about overdoses; drug laws are severe but inconsistently enforced

### Tourist Scene
- **Backpacker popularity:** Legendary - a rite of passage for many travelers
- **Type of tourists:** Party seekers, yoga practitioners, digital nomads, gap year travelers
- **Danger level:** Moderate - drug-related dangers, motorbike accidents, petty theft

## Sensory Environment

### Sounds
- Morning: Roosters, distant chanting from temple, hungover groans, yoga instructions
- Afternoon: Pool splashing, electronic music from various bars starting up, motorbikes
- Night: BASS - inescapable bass from the party, fire dancers' whooshing, screaming/laughing tourists
- Unique sounds: Longtail boats, geckos, the specific "woop woop" siren of the Full Moon Party

### Smells
- Hostel: Incense (trying too hard), sunscreen, chlorine, Thai cooking from kitchen
- Street: Grilled meat, two-stroke engines, frangipani, spilled buckets of alcohol
- Specific to death location: Salt water, smoke from fire pits, the chemical sweat of intoxication

### Textures
- Surfaces: Bamboo furniture, damp towels, gritty sand everywhere, sticky bar surfaces
- Climate feel: Oppressive humidity, constant light sweat, warm ocean water

### Tastes
- Local food: Pad thai, green curry, mango sticky rice, bucket cocktails of mysterious origin
- Hostel offerings: Overpriced smoothie bowls, mediocre Western food, Chang beer towers

### Visual Details
- Colors: Neon paint (Full Moon body painting), turquoise ocean, green jungle, Instagram-filter everything
- Light quality: Harsh tropical sun, golden hour magic, then chaotic party lights
- Distinctive visuals: Fire spinners, UV paint, thousands of people in neon paint, lotus imagery everywhere

After Priya's death, the staff will be ordered to say they "didn't see anything unusual."

## Hostel Atmosphere

### Day Vibe
Recovery and anticipation. Guests sleeping until 2 PM, slowly gathering by the pool. Yoga classes half-empty with hungover participants. People comparing war stories from last night. The countdown to the next party already beginning.

### Night Vibe
Pre-party chaos from 6 PM. Body painting sessions, bucket cocktails, everyone getting "ready." By 9 PM the hostel empties onto the beach. Those who stay behind are either too far gone or saving their energy. The bass vibrates through the walls until dawn.

### Guest Demographics
20-35 age range, mix of gap year travelers, digital nomads, corporate escapees, and "finding themselves" types. Lots of solo travelers. Nationalities: British, Australian, American, German, Scandinavian, Israeli (post-army travelers). Some locals call them "Full Moon zombies."

### Reputation
"THE place for Full Moon" according to dozens of reviews. Also: complaints about noise, drug pusher reviews ("great vibes but be careful"), occasional reviews mentioning "someone got really sick." The hostel aggressively reports negative reviews about safety.

## Practical Details

### Getting There
Ferry from Surat Thani (6 hours) or speedboat from Koh Samui (30 mins). Songthaew (shared taxi) from pier. Many arrive specifically for Full Moon Party and leave immediately after.

### Check-in Process
Chaotic during Full Moon period. Passport copied, deposit taken, wristband issued. Verbal warning about "staying safe" that's become meaningless through repetition. Upsells for party packages.

### Rules
1. No glass on beach
2. Noise curfew 10 AM-2 PM (sort of enforced)
3. No drugs (performatively posted, completely ignored)
4. Hostel "not responsible for personal choices"
5. All guests must sign liability waiver

## Real-World Research

### Accuracy Notes
- Full Moon Party is real and roughly as described
- Drug contamination issues are a genuine concern
- Thai drug laws are severe (death penalty for trafficking) but enforcement is inconsistent
- Koh Phangan has developed a dual personality: wellness retreats and party scene coexisting

### Cultural Sensitivity
- Don't portray Thai people as complicit in drug trade - most locals are troubled by it
- Show the economic complexity - many rely on party tourism while disliking its effects
- Thai medical and emergency services should be shown as competent when involved
- Buddhism is important; Thai characters should be portrayed respectfully
