# Book Identity

## Core Information

**Book Number:** 042
**Title:** One More Hit
**Slug:** overdose
**Category:** Self-Inflicted

## The Death (One Sentence)
A burned-out tech executive seeking escape at a legendary party hostel dies from a fentanyl-laced cocaine overdose, never suspecting the drugs she bought from a "trusted" fellow traveler would be her last purchase.

## Tagline
"She wanted to feel something. She felt everything. Then nothing."

## Themes
1. The illusion of safety in backpacker communities and misplaced trust
2. Wealthy professionals using travel as escapism from mental health crises
3. The global fentanyl crisis reaching even "paradise" destinations

## Tone Balance
- Comedy: 25%
- Horror: 50%
- Satire: 25%

## Target Reader
Travelers who've encountered drug culture in hostels. Anyone who's made risky decisions while seeking escape. People who understand that "party hostels" have a darker underbelly.

## Comparison Titles
- *Requiem for a Dream* meets *The Beach*

## Content Warnings
- Drug use and overdose (graphic)
- Death by overdose
- Mental health crisis/depression
- Contaminated substances
